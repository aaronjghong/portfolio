  2. 02

    Grass

    Live · WebGPU

    A real-time meadow in the browser, with up to a few million individually simulated grass blades on a 10 m × 10 m field. Each blade is an elastic rod solved with XPBD in WebGPU compute shaders, pushed around by a 2D wind simulation. You can press the grass down with your cursor, and the rustle you hear is synthesized from the simulated leaf motion. There's no recorded wind bed.

    Physics
    XPBD rods
    Blades
    Up to millions
    Audio
    16 spatial zones
    Output
    HDR · AgX · TAA

    • WGSL compute

  3. 03

    This site

    You are here

    A portfolio laid out as a small neural network in three.js. Each section is a layer, and moving between them is a forward pass. With sound on, a single tone is transformed by each layer it passes through (harmonics, wind, frequency modulation, echoes) until it resolves into a chord at the output, and every node can be played.
